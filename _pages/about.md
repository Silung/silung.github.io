I am Shilong Zhao, a third-year master’s student at the State Key Lab of AI Safety, Institute of Computing Technology, Chinese Academy of Sciences. I received my bachelor’s degree from the School of Computer Science at Beijing Institute of Technology. My main research interests are LLM Agent and recommendation systems.

GitHub: [https://github.com/Silung](https://github.com/Silung)

### Recent Works

- Shilong Zhao, Qinggang Yang, Yin Zhiyi, Wang Xiaoshi, Zhenxing Chen, Du Su, and Xueqi Cheng. "PersonaAct: Simulating Short-Video Users with Personalized Agents for Counterfactual Filter Bubble Auditing." Arxiv, 2026.

- Du Su, Zhenxing Chen, Shilong Zhao, Yuanhao Liu, Fei Sun, Qi Cao, and Huawei Shen. "AuditAgent: LLM Agent for Risks Auditing in Recommender Systems." AAAI Demo, 2026.

- Shilong Zhao, Fei Sun, Kaike Zhang, Shaoling Jing, Du Su, Zhichao Shi, Zhiyi Yin, Huawei Shen, Xueqi Cheng. "LLM4MEA: Data-free Model Extraction Attacks on Sequential Recommenders via Large Language Models." SIGIR-AP, 2025.

- Hulingxiao He, Wu Yuan, Yidian Huang, Shilong Zhao, Wen Yuan, Hanqing Li. "Learning Unified Representations for Multi-Resolution Face Recognition." BMVC, 2023.
